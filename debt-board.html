    About this board. ipowork (Capnexus Technologies Private Limited) provides debt market intelligence, analytics, investor matching and introductions. ipowork is not an Online Bond Platform Provider or a stock broker and does not execute, clear or settle transactions in securities, or hold client funds or securities. Any transaction, allocation or fund subscription happens only through the relevant SEBI-registered intermediary, arranger, fund manager or platform, under its own documentation, investor eligibility, KYC/AML and suitability process.

    Signals, scores, predicted windows, readiness assessments and investor-fit counts are model estimates built from public and third-party data. They are not credit ratings, research reports, investment advice or offers, and may be incomplete or out of date. The investor intelligence network describes a potential investor universe; only institutions that have opted in receive matched opportunities. Companies shown have not necessarily engaged ipowork. Nothing on this page guarantees funding, allocation, returns, investors or execution.

    Equity, pre-IPO, unlisted, block, bulk and preferential opportunities are shown only to verified institutions, fund houses and arrangers, are not offers to the public, and complete only through the company, the selling shareholder's broker or the appropriate SEBI-registered intermediary. No-name listings reveal the company only to investors who are wall-crossed under the arranger's insider procedures.
