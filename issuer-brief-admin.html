1 · Sector valuation multiples

Listed-peer trailing P/E ranges used for the indicative valuation. One line per sector: sector,low,mid,high,as_of,source. Sector names must match the sector column in the financials. Sectors without a line get no valuation (the portal says so honestly). Saving adds a new version; the latest version is used.

2 · Load financials and publish briefs

One row per company per financial year (at least 3 years for eligibility), amounts in ₹ crore. Columns can be in any order; blanks are fine and show as "not available".

3 · Create access and magic links

One line per person: email,name,firm,role,cins. Role is issuer or ca_cs. List several CINs separated by ; for a group or CA/CS partner. Links last 30 days; running again for the same person gives them a fresh link and their latest company list. Issuer links are separate from MB and investor links and do not open aira.html.

4 · Issuer activity

Who opened their brief, when, and from which campaign. Access requests and early-access sign-ups go to Leads under the Issuer category.
